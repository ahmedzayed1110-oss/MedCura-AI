# Evidence boundaries

| Evidence label | Meaning here | Permitted interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| OBSERVED_PUBLIC | Published observation or attributed workbook transcription. Verification status identifies primary corroboration. | Describe the stated entity, geography, definition and period. |
| DERIVED | Calculation over explicit source values. | Interpret totals and proportions using the named denominator. |
| VENDOR_CLAIM | Product or commercial description attributed to a vendor. | Describe the advertised capability. Independent deployments require separate evidence. |
| SCENARIO | Assumed commercial parameter, allocation or projected outcome. | Examine what follows from assumptions. |
| SYNTHETIC_DEMO | Generated inventory events, demand or benchmark. | Exercise workflows, evaluate software behavior and inspect simulated outcomes. |
| PILOT_MEASURED | Authorized, audited field observations for a pre-specified branch and window. | Interpret eligible outcomes after completeness and comparability checks. |
| UNKNOWN | Unavailable, incomparable, unverified or undated for the selected question. | Show the gap and specify the evidence required. |

The sources contain aggregate regional evidence. Measuring stockouts, expiry, ordering effort or ROI requires SKU-day transactions and aligned baseline and follow-up observations. A 2024 private-complex count concerns facility locations. The relationship between locations and purchasing accounts requires commercial research. The market data are unsuitable as a training series for clinic demand.

A primary observation takes precedence only when entity, definition, geography and period match. Workbook “Verified” strings, claims of market exclusivity and proposed policy alignment provide no independent verification. Every aggregate metric points back to input cells. The original wording remains available in the cell snapshots.

A file's schema and a successful upload cannot establish permission to use real operational records. A pilot needs branch authorization, an agreed window, approved provenance, an evidence owner and reconciliation. Manual transactions entered into the demo branch remain synthetic.
