# Product and completion criteria

The intended user is a Saudi private medical-complex branch manager responsible for non-pharmaceutical consumables. The initial chain demonstration covers gloves, masks and dressings, with SKU-specific expiry rules. Owners review market evidence, approve movements, and examine hypothetical economics. Pilot leads register future measurement and eligibility.

| Module | Reviewable outcome |
|---|---|
| M1 | Rank and compare all 13 administrative regions with exact cells, years and national denominators |
| M2 | Inspect discrepancies, preserve missing values and inspect evidence status before reuse |
| M3 | Receive packs, issue base units via FEFO, reserve, reconcile and reconstruct physical stock after restart |
| M4 | Inspect history coverage, rolling holdout metrics, policy assumptions and a reproducible reorder decision |
| M5 | Quantify a shelf-life-constrained candidate, approve it, and reconcile inventory in transit and at receipt |
| M6 | Save editable cases from reachable chains through paying branches to financial outputs and gates |
| M7 | Register a one-branch prospective protocol, validate coded input and enforce enrollment before measured claims |
| M8 | Present a 10-slide narrative with source captions, quantitative figures, notes, Q&A and research report |

The training workflow is local, in English, with browser-native keyboard interaction and responsive navigation. The model excludes patient records, clinical decisions, pharmaceutical ordering and external procurement. Claims about forecast quality, expiry savings, adoption or conversion require the corresponding real records and authorization.
