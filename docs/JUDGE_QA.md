# Questions from the judging panel

## Why Riyadh first?

The 2024 workbook ranks Riyadh first by private complexes. A single-site pilot also gives the team a bounded place to reconcile records and compare the workflow with a baseline.

## How many buyers are reachable?

Unknown. The facility data omit parent chain and purchasing authority. Obtain permission to map branches to decision-makers and validate qualified leads independently.

## Can the forecast use regional population?

The operational model requires dated branch and SKU usage. A facility-to-population ratio can describe geography, while unknown visits, case mix and consumption prevent it from estimating item demand.

## Why compare simple forecasting methods?

A repeatable seasonal or trailing baseline establishes whether complexity adds value on earlier unseen periods. Report the coverage, horizon, sample and error on later observed dates.

## What happens to missing observations?

Keep them missing. A genuine zero requires a recorded observation with zero usage. Stockouts can censor demand even when recorded usage is low.

## Does the system order automatically?

Purchase and transfer proposals require authorized review. A confirmed order affects expected inbound stock until the goods are physically received.

## How do transfers conserve units?

Reserve at request, deduct at dispatch and track goods in transit. Post the destination lot at receipt exactly once. Check remaining shelf life and how fast the receiving site can use it.

## How much money does this save?

There is no measured savings estimate. Enter assumed costs and conversion in the scenario model. A field estimate requires comparable operating windows and the full implementation cost.

## How does the product differ from existing tools?

The hypotheses are lightweight integration, visible evidence and reviewable inter-branch decisions. Compare these in buyer interviews and trials. Vendor advertising alone cannot establish local deployment or relative superiority.

## Can we call a pre/post difference an intervention effect?

A single-branch comparison is vulnerable to season, staffing and supplier changes. Record these factors and use a comparable branch or staggered rollout when feasible.

## Has a regulator endorsed the product?

The supplied guide describes a strategic interpretation. Any pilot must undergo its own privacy, cybersecurity, supplier, hosting and classification reviews.
