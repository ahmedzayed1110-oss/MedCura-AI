# Regulatory questions before a partner pilot

This is a planning register for a prospective Saudi private medical-complex partner. The prototype uses synthetic branch transactions. Site-specific classification, contracts and sign-off remain open.

| Decision owner | Question to resolve | Evidence required before field use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clinic privacy lead and counsel | Could staff identities, approval records or linked operational logs identify an individual? | Data inventory, lawful purpose, processing roles, minimization rules, retention and deletion schedule. |
| Clinic security and hosting owners | Which systems, administrators, backups and subprocessors can access the operational records? | Approved deployment boundary, hosting locations, access matrix, audit retention, incident procedures and recovery test. |
| Privacy lead and counsel | Would support, storage, telemetry or remote access involve a cross-border transfer? | Documented data flows and specialist assessment of transfer conditions and any safeguards. |
| Clinic and supplier | Which product lots require recorded expiry, transport conditions, quarantine or recall handling? | A reviewed SKU-specific policy and supplier evidence. Resolve health-product classification before expanding the catalog. |
| Procurement and information security | Do contract terms or the partner's status bring particular cybersecurity controls into scope? | Applicable requirements list with owner and review date. Check the NCA scope and any sector or contractual requirements. |
| Site sponsor | Who can authorize a trial, approve a transfer and publish aggregate findings? | Signed pilot protocol, authorized users, escalation process and disclosure approval. |

The review should cover staff audit metadata even when uploaded inventory files omit patient fields. Keep patient identifiers outside this product's dataset. Treat policy alignment in the presentation guide as a strategic interpretation. The partner must verify current applicability and obtain appropriate advice before processing live data.

The Saudi Data & AI Authority publishes the personal data protection regime, implementing regulations and transfer guidance. [SDAIA implementing regulation](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPL2/)

The National Cybersecurity Authority describes the applicable entity scope of its essential controls and publishes cloud controls. The partner should assess scope against its own infrastructure and contracts. [NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/ecc/) · [NCA Cloud Cybersecurity Controls](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/ccc/)
